Prompt Firewall popup concepts

Three directions for the toolbar popup. The popup stays small and answers three questions: is it on here, what did it catch, and what can I change fast. Editing and review happen on the page. Every concept uses the same redaction-bar language for severity: a solid bar is redacted high severity, a slate bar is redacted medium, and a dotted underline is highlighted only.
